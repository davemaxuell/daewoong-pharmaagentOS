# Intended use and regulated-use boundary

Status: starter baseline pending formal Daewoong QA/System Owner approval.

The service is an internal regulatory-intelligence and decision-support aid. It helps authorized staff monitor public FDA `Product: Drugs` warning letters, inspect official evidence, compare source-backed themes, and retrieve cited passages. The official FDA source remains authoritative.

The service does not autonomously determine Daewoong compliance, create or approve a CAPA, change an SOP, release or reject product, control manufacturing or laboratory equipment, submit content to a regulator, or replace qualified human review. AI summaries, attention levels, taxonomy labels, and comparison questions are derived aids and must remain visually distinct from source facts.

Formal re-assessment is required before the service or any output becomes a GxP record, required quality-system evidence, an automated compliance decision, an input that directly controls a regulated process, or an integration that writes to validated quality/manufacturing systems. That assessment determines validation, electronic-record/signature, retention, change-control, audit-trail, supplier, and periodic-review requirements under Daewoong procedures.

Production release requires an approved intended-use assessment, named System Owner and business owner, data/retention classification, access review, vendor decisions, restore evidence, and acceptance of all residual risks with owner and expiry.
